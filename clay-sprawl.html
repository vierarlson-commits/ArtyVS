サイバーパンク · A FIELD GUIDE

High tech.Low life.

Cyberpunk is science fiction about a near future that went sideways. The machines are dazzling, the cities are broken, and the people at the bottom wire the two together. The city behind this page is sculpted from digital clay. Scroll to walk around it.

MOTIFS

Six things every cyberpunk city is built from

  • MegacorporationsCompanies that outgrew governments and now run the police, the news and the weather.
  • The sprawlCities fused into one endless, vertical, rain-slick neighbourhood.
  • CyberspaceData as a place you can jack into, walk through and rob.
  • Body modificationChrome arms, mirrored eyes, and the question of how much of you is left.
  • Artificial mindsAIs and replicants who want what people want: more life, fewer owners.
  • The streetHackers, couriers and fixers who repurpose corporate tech for their own ends.

TIMELINE

How the genre got wired

  1. 1980
    The word is coinedBruce Bethke writes the short story “Cyberpunk”. It reaches print in Amazing Stories in 1983.
  2. 1982
    Blade Runner and AkiraRidley Scott’s rain-soaked Los Angeles sets the look. Katsuhiro Otomo’s Neo-Tokyo manga begins the same year.
  3. 1984
    NeuromancerWilliam Gibson’s debut novel makes “cyberspace” a household word and sweeps the Hugo, Nebula and Philip K. Dick awards.
  4. 1986
    MirrorshadesBruce Sterling’s anthology gathers the movement’s writers under one cover and gives it a manifesto.
  5. 1988
    Dice hit the tableMike Pondsmith publishes the Cyberpunk role-playing game. Akira arrives in cinemas.
  6. 1992
    Snow CrashNeal Stephenson turns the genre into satire and names the Metaverse.
  7. 1995
    Ghost in the ShellMamoru Oshii’s film asks where a mind ends once the body is fully manufactured.
  8. 1999
    The MatrixThe Wachowskis bring simulated reality and black trench coats to the mainstream.
  9. 2020
    Cyberpunk 2077Pondsmith’s Night City becomes an open-world video game, three decades after the tabletop original.

OFFSHOOTS

The punks that came after

Post-cyberpunk
Same technology, but the heroes have jobs, families and a stake in the system.
Biopunk
Swaps the soldering iron for gene splicing. The body is the hardware.
Steampunk
Runs the same thought experiment backwards, on brass and coal.
Solarpunk
The optimistic reply: green cities, shared power, a future worth wanting.
